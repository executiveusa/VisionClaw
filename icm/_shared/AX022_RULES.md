# AX-022 Shared Rules

These rules apply to every AX-022 mission.

1. Verify before claiming.
2. Cash/proof before speculative code.
3. Inspect before changing.
4. Reuse before adding.
5. Specify before building.
6. One verifiable slice at a time.
7. Builders cannot approve themselves.
8. Preserve owner control of code, hosting, credentials, customer data, and device integrations.
9. Keep device adapters replaceable; do not hard-wire the product to one glasses vendor.
10. Glasses are a presence/I-O layer. Business authority remains server-side.
11. Jarvis is the intended ambient device/glasses gateway; business missions route onward to Hermes under the fleet contract.
12. No secrets in client apps, glasses firmware, repository docs, screenshots, logs, or examples.
13. Every consequential release must have rollback and evidence.
