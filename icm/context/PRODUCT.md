# Product — AI GLASSES AX-022

## Outcome

Turn smart glasses into a custom, voice-first and vision-aware interface for an agentic business system: hear the user, see approved visual context, speak privately back, display concise information when hardware supports it, and route authorized actions to existing agents/tools.

## Commercial product

Do not sell "smart glasses" alone. Sell a configured business operating system bundled with supported glasses, onboarding, company knowledge, workflows, integrations, translation, approvals, and ongoing operations.

## Current repository role

`executiveusa/VisionClaw` is the current implementation base. It already contains Meta Ray-Ban + phone pathways for real-time voice/vision and tool execution. Preserve those working paths while extracting vendor-neutral contracts.

## Intended hardware strategy

- Brilliant Labs Halo — preferred first open hardware target; PLANNED until implemented and verified here.
- Meta Ray-Ban — current implementation base.
- Mentra — candidate adapter target.
- Rokid — candidate commercial adapter target.

## Primary user experiences

- Ask status: "What needs me?"
- Talk to Jarvis and route to Hermes/business systems.
- Capture what the user sees and create a bounded mission.
- Hear private responses through the glasses.
- Show approvals/status on a HUD when supported.
- Translate conversations.
- Approve/reject consequential actions through explicit controls.

## Non-goals

- Put all business logic or credentials on the glasses.
- Duplicate Hermes/STARNET/Pauli's Place/MAXX inside the wearable app.
- Claim hardware support before proof.
